THE PROBLEM

Healthcare shouldn't start with repeating the same questions.

Hospitals handle large patient volumes while critical information often remains scattered across conversations, papers and old reports.

🏥

High Patient Load

Crowded hospitals create pressure on doctors and healthcare staff.

⏱

Time-Consuming Intake

Manual history-taking consumes valuable consultation time.

🔁

Repetitive Questions

Patients repeatedly provide the same information to different staff members.

📄

Scattered Records

Prescriptions and medical reports are often stored as physical documents.

✍

Hard-to-Read Records

Handwritten records can be difficult to quickly interpret.

⚠

Missed Information

Important history may not be immediately visible during consultation.
